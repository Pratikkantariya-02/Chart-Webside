Bar Chart - Sales Comparison

Bar Chart: Compares monthly sales across different years. Use the dropdown to switch datasets for each year.

Line Chart - Revenue

Line Chart: Shows revenue trends over months. Toggle between years to compare seasonal patterns.

Pie Chart - Color Distribution

Pie Chart: Visualizes distribution of categories (colors) as parts of a whole. Useful to see proportionate shares.

Doughnut Chart - Category Distribution

Doughnut Chart: Breaks down categories such as product types or customer segments and their relative sizes.

Radar Chart - Player Performance

Radar Chart: Compares player attributes (e.g., speed, strength, skill) on a radial axis to show strengths and weaknesses.
